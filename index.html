(About)

Few notes.
A lot of soul

My sound blends punchy drums with soul, jazz and blues samples, rooted in the music my father played for me growing up. That early influence shaped an ear tuned to nuance, depth and feeling.

The beats move between cinematic atmospheres and darker shades when the mood calls for it, always guided by a minimalist, precise approach to drums. You can hear echoes of Metro Boomin, Kanye West, Pharrell, Southside and Nick Mira, but the result is unmistakably my own.

Today I work with emerging artists across the U.S., France, Italy and the U.K., shaping a sound that aims to connect before anything else, for people looking for something that actually stirs something inside, not just another beat.
